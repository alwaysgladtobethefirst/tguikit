8 new components, closing out page-navigation and mobile-catalog patterns.

- **Steps** – compact segmented progress bar (`count`, `progress`, fractional fill), like a Stories bar
- **Timeline** – vertical event list with a connector rail; `active` marks the current node, later items read as pending
- **Breadcrumbs** – truncating page trail, collapses the middle past `maxItems`, `aria-current="page"` on the tail
- **Pagination** – numbered page control with prev/next and ellipsis truncation
- **CompactPagination** – dot variant of `Pagination`, windowed for large counts
- **InlineButtons** – row of icon-over-label actions (call/video/mute pattern), shares width evenly, scrolls past a threshold
- **SearchBar** – iOS-style pill search input, clear button, optional fade-in Cancel on focus
- **Stepper** – plus/minus quantity control with an editable, clamped value
